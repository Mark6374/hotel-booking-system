## 2. What was added

### 2.1 Attempts counter
Every wrong password now says how many tries are left before the 15-minute lock (week 12):

> Invalid email or password. **3 attempts left** before the account is locked.
> Неверный email или пароль. **До блокировки осталось 3 попытки.**
> Email немесе құпиясөз қате. **Аккаунт бұғатталғанға дейін 3 әрекет қалды.**

An **unknown e-mail gets exactly the same answers** (4, 3, 2, 1 left, then "locked"), so nobody can find out which e-mails have an account. This keeps the team's rule from `tests.py` (*wrong password and unknown e-mail give the same error*). For unknown e-mails nothing is written to the database; the count lives in the Django cache for 15 minutes.

### 2.2 "Someone tried to log in to your account"
If wrong passwords were typed for an account since its last successful login, then after the next login the owner sees a red notice at the top of the page:

> **Someone tried to log in to your account**
> 5 wrong passwords were typed for your account since your last login. The last try was on 27 Nov 2026, 14:02.
> Because of this the account was locked for 15 minutes.
> **If this was not you, change your password now.**
> [Change password] [It was me]

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Change password** | opens *My account → Password*; after the password is changed the notice disappears |
| **It was me** | hides the notice; it does not come back at the next login |

The notice is shown once (the record is removed at login), only to the owner of that account, in English, Russian and Kazakh. If the admin unlocks the account, the owner still gets the notice.
